Website + Booking System · EspaiFest · Martorell

An event venue that stopped managing bookings over WhatsApp and automated the whole process

Laura ran her celebrations venue in Martorell handling every inquiry by hand: WhatsApp, calls, emails. With no website or booking system, the process was slow and she couldn't switch off. Local word of mouth worked, but there was no way to scale.

We built the EspaiFest website in two languages (Catalan and Spanish) with all the venue's information, rates and extras. We integrated Reservify, our booking system, so families and businesses could check availability and book directly, with automatic date locking and instant confirmation.

"I used to spend the whole day answering the same WhatsApp message over and over. Now the website explains everything and the system manages availability on its own. If there's a free date, it gets booked. If not, it doesn't. As simple as that."

— Laura · Owner, EspaiFest Martorell · espaifest.com

Meta Ads Advertising · Luxinpro · Menorca and the Balearic Islands

A luxury Project & Building Manager that started attracting European clients before they even reached the island

Luxinpro has spent more than 15 years managing exclusive real estate projects in Menorca and the Balearics: new builds, full renovations, SmartHome and complete project management for a demanding international clientele. Creatives adapted to the client's international profile, segmentation by key countries (Germany, France, UK) and optimization for lead quality, not just volume.

Lead-generation website · Finca S'Espinal · Menorca

An owner who wanted to sell his rural estate in Menorca and needed to reach buyers across Europe

The owner of Finca S'Espinal — a 30,000 m² rural estate in Sant Climent, Menorca, built in 1891 with a traditional Menorcan farmhouse, pool and 15 auxiliary structures — wanted to sell the property, but without a digital presence it was invisible to national and international buyers. The ideal buyer profile doesn't search for estates on the local market: they search Google from Germany, France or Barcelona.

We designed a website dedicated exclusively to the property: professional gallery, complete technical sheet, an investment section with Balearic real estate market data, and a contact form to request the dossier. SEO optimized specifically for estate-for-sale searches in Menorca, in Spanish and structured to capture international traffic.

"I wanted to sell the estate but didn't know how to reach buyers outside Menorca. With the website we started getting inquiries from interested people who had found us on Google. Without it, the property was completely invisible."

— Owner, Finca S'Espinal · fincasespinalmenorca.com
